Executive Briefing

Malachyte has leveraged its ex-Spotify founder story and $10M raise to establish strong market posture in e-commerce personalization. However, the website currently exhibits severe conversion leaks, including no Meta/LinkedIn retargeting pixels, absent Schema.org JSON-LD data for AI search indexing (Generative Engine Optimization), and missing homepage H1 tags. Addressing these quick-fix technical gaps will instantly optimize their inbound traffic conversion and expand organic AI discovery.

Identified Weaknesses & Growth Impact
  • Missing OpenGraph social preview image
    Impact: Links shared on LinkedIn, Slack, and X display as blank, text-only cards.
  • Missing <h1> heading tag on homepage
    Impact: Confuses Google crawlers regarding your primary category and dilutes top keyword rankings.
  • 18 of 40 images missing alt text
    Impact: Degrades accessibility and misses high-intent Google Image search traffic.
  • No Schema.org / JSON-LD structured data found
    Impact: Invisibility in modern AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude), preventing AI answer bots from recommending your product.
  • No Meta Pixel detected
    Impact: Non-converting website visitors bounce unmonetized; company cannot run high-ROI paid social retargeting on Instagram/Facebook to recapture warm leads.
  • Missing or misconfigured robots.txt file
    Impact: Fails to guide search engine crawl budgets and risks indexing administrative routes.
  • No active marketing job openings despite having a careers portal
    Impact: Signals lean commercial headcount ripe for fractional campaign execution.
