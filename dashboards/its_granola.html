What They Do & Core Value Proposition

Its Granola is a Saudi D2C health-food brand selling artisan, locally baked granola across six custom flavors directly through a Salla e-commerce store. They target health-conscious Saudi millennials, modern households, and fitness enthusiasts seeking premium, guilt-free breakfast and snacking options.

Commercial Market Posture

Its Granola has built an authentic homegrown founder narrative in Saudi Arabia, but suffers from a critical void in short-form video execution. With a virtually dormant TikTok presence and an Instagram feed bound to static carousels and long text captions, the brand is leaking high-intent top-of-funnel traffic in a booming D2C health food sector.

Executive Briefing

Its Granola has established strong product authority and compelling narrative roots, but its digital distribution strategy is anchored in outdated static formats. In the competitive KSA food and wellness space, visual aesthetic video—specifically ASMR recipe builds, founder-led BTS, and lifestyle coffee pairing—is mandatory to drive scale. By deploying our specialized agency video engine across TikTok and Reels, Its Granola can double its Salla conversion rate and capture dominant category mindshare in Riyadh and across the GCC.
