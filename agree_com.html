Executive Briefing

Agree.com recently secured $7.2M to disrupt the contract-to-cash ecosystem, establishing strong category momentum. Despite this backing, the company exhibits critical technical conversion leaks: it currently operates without Schema.org JSON-LD structured data—rendering it invisible to modern AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity—and lacks a Meta retargeting pixel to nurture bouncing site traffic. Furthermore, the website features 5 competing H1 tags and lacks an active blog, diluting search engine ranking signals and forfeiting compounding organic acquisition.

  • Multiple (5) <h1> heading tags found

    Impact: Degrades accessibility and misses high-intent Google Image search traffic.
  • No Schema.org / JSON-LD structured data found
    Impact: Invisibility in modern AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude), preventing AI answer bots from recommending your product.

    Impact: Non-converting website visitors bounce unmonetized; company cannot run high-ROI paid social retargeting on Instagram/Facebook to recapture warm leads.

  • No active blog or content hub detected
    Impact: Forfeits compounding organic search equity and top-of-funnel educational search traffic.
