Identified Weaknesses & Growth Impact
  • Missing <title> tag on homepage
    Impact: Forfeits primary browser tab branding and search engine snippet headline.
  • Missing meta description
    Impact: Google generates random text snippets, lowering click-through rates from search results.
  • Missing OpenGraph social preview image
    Impact: Links shared on LinkedIn, Slack, and X display as blank, text-only cards.
  • Missing <h1> heading tag on homepage
    Impact: Confuses Google crawlers regarding your primary category and dilutes top keyword rankings.
  • Missing mobile <meta name='viewport'> tag
    Impact: Causes layout breaking and poor responsiveness on mobile devices.
  • No Schema.org / JSON-LD structured data found
    Impact: Invisibility in modern AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude), preventing AI answer bots from recommending your product.
  • No visible lead capture form or demo scheduling CTA detected on homepage
    Impact: High-intent inbound visitors land on the domain with no clear conversion funnel, resulting in wasted acquisition spend.
  • No Meta Pixel detected
    Impact: Non-converting website visitors bounce unmonetized; company cannot run high-ROI paid social retargeting on Instagram/Facebook to recapture warm leads.
  • No active blog or content hub detected
    Impact: Forfeits compounding organic search equity and top-of-funnel educational search traffic.
  • Missing Linkedin presence
    Impact: Untapped channel for B2B institutional credibility & executive thought leadership.
  • Missing Youtube presence
    Impact: Untapped channel for Long-form product demos & search discovery.
  • Missing X presence
    Impact: Untapped channel for Industry commentary & founder branding.
